## Coverage of the proposal's criteria

Every criterion with a visible outcome lands on a screen row: FR-1 (dialog, add control), FR-2 (author, poem, collection rows), FR-3 (`/admin` row), FR-4 and FR-5 (list row), FR-6 and FR-11 (warning dialog), FR-7 and FR-8 (row actions, early-block row), FR-9 and FR-14 (result lines, author page after a delete), FR-15 (failure and `offline` states in every row). FR-12 and FR-13 have no screen.

Gap found: FR-10's visible outcome (a case leaves the list once its user is blocked or banned) is not drawn. The result frames show the success line inside the row but not whether the row stays or goes on the next render. To confirm with the owner, then record in the component's behaviour: the design reads it as "the row disappears on the next render, as for «Случая больше нет»".
