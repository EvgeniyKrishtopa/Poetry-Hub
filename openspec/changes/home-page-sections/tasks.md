# Tasks

Order: group 1 is the database (a spike on tag propagation first, then the two tables and the guarded functions). Group 2 adds the two new public reads to `poems`. Group 3 composes the home data in `home` (ids, authors, poems, latest) with every failure as a value. Group 4 is the admin's write side (RPC wrappers, actions, the `AddToHomeControl`, its wiring into the poem and author pages). Group 5 is the `/admin/home` page and its breadcrumb. Group 6 is the six home sections, the hero image and the new `HomePage`; it is the only group with user-facing screens, so the Claude Design brief is written after Gate 2 and the test plan and recorded in `PROGRESS.md`, and group 6 does not start until the approved link arrives (`docs/claude-design.md`); groups 1 to 5 are non-UI except the admin control and page, which reuse the existing admin primitives. Group 7 is the docs, the deploy checklist and the final verification. Each group lands its own tests and docs. Names avoid the substrings that `home-greeting` FR-10 bans (`PoemList`, `PoemCard`, …). A throwaway spike is undone by restoring backed-up copies from `$TMPDIR`, never by `git checkout`.
